Muse Sleep — Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Muse Sleep is a personal application operated by Ethan McConkie for his own individual use. It is not a commercial service and has no other users.

Data accessed

With the account holder's consent, granted through WHOOP's OAuth flow, this application reads his WHOOP data: sleep, recovery, cycle, and workout records (scopes read:sleep, read:recovery, read:cycles, read:workout), plus a refresh token (offline scope) so the connection continues without repeated sign-ins.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the account holder his own sleep and health information in his personal tools (his Sleep & Morning dashboard and his Home Base display) and to trigger his own morning notifications when a sleep is scored.

Sharing

The data is never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising or analytics.

Storage and security

Data is stored only in the account holder's own systems and devices. OAuth credentials are held in secure credential storage; the application itself cannot read them back.

Revocation and deletion

The account holder may revoke this application's access at any time in his WHOOP settings, which stops all further data access. Stored data is deleted on request, or when access is revoked.
